Welcome to the Cozy World of Thread & Love!

At Thread & Love, we believe that every home deserves a touch of handmade warmth and cozy comfort. Founded in a tiny garden workshop in Henley-in-Arden, our passion is crafting premium, eco-friendly crochet items that tell a story. From lovable amigurumi plushies and retro daisy tote bags to chunky pastel cardigans and rustic living decor, each piece is looped by hand with meticulous care and slow-craft devotion. We source 100% certified organic cotton yarns and natural dyes, advocating for sustainable, slow fashion that respects both the artisan and the Earth. Welcome to our family; let's stay warm together.
