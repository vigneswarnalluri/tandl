  • Slow Crafted with Love Every single Retro Daisy Flower Tote Bag is individually hand-crocheted by local artisans at the Thread & Love studio. We practice "slow fashion," meaning we don't rush or mass-produce. Each bag takes between 6 to 8 hours of dedicated handwork, looping premium organic cotton yarn into durable, beautiful granny squares that celebrate vintage styles with a modern aesthetic.
  • Sustainable & Organic We utilize 100% certified organic cotton yarns, sourced ethically and dyed with natural, chemical-free pigments. This gives our tote bags an incredibly soft feel, natural durability, and ensures they are entirely biodegradable.
  • Zero-Waste Philosophy As a crochet selling business, our production process yields virtually zero textile waste. Every scrap of yarn is collected and repurposed into stuffing for our Amigurumi toys or accent stitching, making this a truly eco-friendly purchase.
  • Unique & Boutique Because each item is crafted completely by hand, minor variations in stitching and sizing may occur. This is the hallmark of genuine hand-crocheted work, making your Thread & Love tote uniquely yours!
